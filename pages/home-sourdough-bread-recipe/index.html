Introduction to Sourdough Bread

Sourdough bread is a type of bread made using naturally occurring yeast and lactic acid bacteria. Unlike conventional bread, sourdough fermentation provides a unique flavor and texture while also offering several health benefits, including better digestibility and lower gluten content.

Ingredients Required

  • 500 grams of bread flour
  • 350 ml of water (room temperature)
  • 100 grams of active sourdough starter
  • 10 grams of salt

Tools Needed

  • Mixing bowl
  • Bench scraper
  • Kitchen scale
  • Dutch oven or baking stone
  • Proofing basket
  • Sharp knife or lame for scoring

How to Prepare the Sourdough Starter

Feeding Your Starter

To have a successful sourdough bread, you need a well-fed starter. Follow these steps:

  1. Feed your sourdough starter with equal weights of flour and water (e.g., 50g of each).
  2. Let it sit at room temperature for 4-6 hours until it bubbles and doubles in size.
  3. If not used immediately, refrigerate your starter and feed it weekly.

Mixing the Dough

  1. In a large mixing bowl, combine the flour and salt.
  2. In another bowl, mix the water and sourdough starter until dissolved.
  3. Add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients, stirring until no dry flour remains.
  4. Cover with a cloth and let it rest for 30 minutes (autolyse).

Stretch and Fold

After the autolyse, perform a series of stretch and folds to develop gluten:

  1. Wet your hands to prevent sticking.
  2. Gently pull one side of the dough up and fold it over itself.
  3. Repeat on all four sides of the dough.
  4. Cover and let it rest for 30 minutes.
  5. Repeat the process 3 to 4 times over 2 hours.

Shaping the Dough

Once the dough has risen satisfactorily:

  1. Transfer it to a lightly floured surface.
  2. Gently shape it into a round or oval form, being careful not to deflate it.
  3. Place it in a proofing basket for the final rise (about 2 hours at room temperature or overnight in the fridge).

Baking the Bread

It’s time to bake!

  1. Preheat your oven to 230°C (450°F) with the Dutch oven inside for about 30 minutes.
  2. Carefully remove the hot Dutch oven and place the dough inside.
  3. Cover and bake for 30 minutes. Remove the lid; bake for an additional 15-20 minutes until golden brown.
  4. Let the bread cool on a wire rack before slicing.

Tips for Perfect Sourdough

  • Keep a consistent feeding schedule for your starter.
  • Experiment with different hydration levels of flour for varied textures.
  • Use a kitchen scale for precise measurements.
  • Be patient; sourdough takes practice to perfect!

Conclusion

With practice and patience, baking sourdough bread can become a fulfilling and tasty experience. Enjoy the journey, and don't forget to share your bread with friends and family!
