The Coffee Pot Trick

What You Need

  • Your regular coffee pot
  • A fresh batch of coffee
  • A sock
  • Hot water

The Hack

Next time you want to brew coffee but can’t find a coffee filter, just take an old sock (clean, of course) and place your coffee grounds inside it. Tie it off and submerge it in hot water like a tea bag. Voilà! Fresh coffee without a filter! Not only does it provide a smooth brew, but you'll also ensure you have coffee without the hassle of running out.

Banana Peel Shoe Shine

What You Need

  • A banana
  • Your favorite pair of shoes
  • A soft cloth

How It Works

This may sound silly, but banana peels contain potassium and natural oils that can help to clean and shine your shoes. Simply rub the inside of a banana peel over your leather shoes, then use a soft cloth to buff them to a brilliant shine. Not only is it effective, but it’s also a great way to reduce waste!

Sticky Note Bookmarkers

This Hack Is Perfect For...

If you love reading but can never find your bookmarks, you can use sticky notes instead! Not only are they bright and colorful, but you can also write notes on them about your thoughts regarding the book.

How to Use

Just peel off a sticky note and place it on the current page you’re on. You can even write down a quote or a thought you had while reading for future reference. Easy and organized!

Egg Carton Paint Palette

For Creative Souls

If you enjoy painting, you might find yourself needing a palette for your colors. Instead of spending money on a fancy palette, simply use an empty egg carton! The cups of the carton serve as perfect little wells for your paint, and once you’re finished, you can just toss it in the recycling.

Whether using a sock to brew coffee, banana peels for shine, or sticky notes for bookmarks, it’s all about making life a bit easier and a lot more humorous. Try these out, and let us know which ridiculous household hack brought a smile to your face!
