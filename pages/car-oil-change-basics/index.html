Car Oil Change Basics

Why Oil Changes Are Important

Regular oil changes are crucial for maintaining your car's engine health. Engine oil lubricates the moving parts, reducing friction and preventing overheating. Over time, oil degrades and becomes less effective, potentially leading to engine problems.

When to Change the Oil

Most manufacturers recommend changing the oil every 3,000 to 5,000 miles; however, some newer models can go up to 7,500 miles or more. Always consult your owner’s manual for the best guidance relevant to your specific vehicle.

Required Tools and Materials

Tools

  • Wrench or socket set
  • Oil filter wrench
  • Oil catch pan
  • Funnel
  • Jack and jack stands or ramps

Materials

  • New oil (check for the correct type and viscosity)
  • New oil filter
  • Rags for clean-up
  • Optional: Oil additives

Step-by-Step Process

Step 1: Prepare Your Vehicle

Park your vehicle on a level surface and switch off the engine. Allow it to cool for a few minutes to avoid burns when handling the oil.

Step 2: Lift the Vehicle

Use a jack to lift your vehicle and secure it with jack stands. If using ramps, ensure they are stable before driving up.

Step 3: Drain the Old Oil

Locate the oil drain plug underneath the vehicle. Use your wrench to loosen and remove the drain plug while directing the used oil into the catch pan. Let it drain completely.

Step 4: Replace the Oil Filter

Using the oil filter wrench, loosen the old oil filter. Apply a small amount of new oil to the gasket of the new filter (to ensure a good seal) and install it, tightening securely by hand.

Step 5: Add New Oil

Replace the drain plug securely. Open the hood and remove the oil filler cap. Using a funnel, pour in the appropriate amount of new oil (refer to your owner’s manual for the specific quantity).

Step 6: Check Oil Level

Start your engine and let it run for a minute. Then, turn it off, and check the oil level using the dipstick. Add more oil if necessary.

Disposing of Old Oil

Do not dispose of used motor oil in the trash or down the drain. Instead, take it to a local recycling center or authorized disposal site.

Conclusion

Changing your car's oil is a simple maintenance task that contributes significantly to its longevity and performance. By following these steps and performing regular checks, you can keep your engine running smoothly.
