Dumb Ways to Use Paperclips

Creative Uses for Paperclips

Paperclips are not just for holding together important documents. This versatile item can be found in every office and home, yet its potential often goes unrecognized. Here, we explore innovative and sometimes silly ways to use paperclips beyond their traditional use.

1. Bookmark Replacement

If you have run out of bookmarks, paperclips can step in as a perfect substitute. They’re just the right size to hold your place in a book, and you can even color-code them for different sections or chapters.

2. Zipper Pulls

A broken zipper pull can be frustrating. Simply bend a paperclip into a hook and attach it to the zipper slider. Not only does this get your zipper functioning again, but it adds a quirky touch to your jacket or bag.

3. SIM Card Ejector

Lost your SIM card eject tool? No problem! The end of a paperclip can easily pop the SIM card tray out of your phone. Just make sure to straighten one end, apply a little pressure, and voilà!

4. Cable Organizer

Papers are not the only things that get tangled! Use a paperclip to keep your cables organized. Simply wrap the cable around a few paperclips attached to your desk, and keep those charging cables from creating a chaotic mess.

5. Keyring Substitute

Have keys that need to be grouped but no keyring on hand? A paperclip can function as a temporary solution. Simply twist two paperclips together to form an improvised keyring until you can replace it.

6. Phone Stand

A quick and easy phone stand can be fashioned from paperclips. By bending them into an appropriate shape, you can prop your phone up for watching videos hands-free. It’s perfect for making calls or watching movies without straining your hands.

7. Picture Hanger

Instead of using traditional picture hangers, use paperclips! All you need to do is shape them into hooks and attach them to the corners of your photos or cards, then stick them up on a surface. It creates a unique display.

8. Twist Tie for Snacks

If you’ve opened a bag of snacks but don’t have a twist tie handy, a paperclip can work wonders. Just fold the top of the bag over and secure it with a paperclip. Your snacks will stay fresh until you’re ready to dig in again!

9. Travel Toothpaste Squeezer

When traveling with toothpaste, a paperclip can help you squeeze out the last drops. Attach it to the end of the toothpaste tube, and roll it up slightly as you use it. This simple hack ensures you don’t waste product!

10. Earbud Holder

Hate untangling earbud wires? Use a paperclip to keep them organized. Simply wrap the cords around the paperclip to prevent knots and keep them tangle-free when stored in your bag. Not only does it protect your earbuds, but it also makes them easier to access.

Conclusion

Paperclips are useful in various ingenious ways that you might not have considered. Keep a few on hand, and don’t hesitate to flex your creativity when it comes to everyday problems. With these clever hacks stored in your resource arsenal, you'll be adapting paperclips for clever uses in no time!
