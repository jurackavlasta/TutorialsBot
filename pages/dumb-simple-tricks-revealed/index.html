Silly Hacks for Everyday Life

1. Unlocking a Door with a Credit Card

Have you ever locked yourself out of your room or car? Instead of calling for help or waiting for a locksmith, you can use a credit card to unlock some doors! While this may not work for all types of locks, it’s a fun trick for many standard latch locks. Just slide the card between the door and frame to push the latch back.

2. Use a Straw to Hull Strawberries

Cleaning strawberries can be a hassle, especially if you have to remove the green tops. A quick way to hull strawberries is to use a drinking straw. Simply push the straw through the base of the strawberry and pop the hull out – quick and easy!

3. Shoe Drying via Rice

If you've ever ended up with wet shoes because you forgot your umbrella on a rainy day, here is a simple hack. Fill a bag with dry rice and stuff it into your wet shoes. Leave it overnight, and the rice will absorb moisture, leaving your shoes nice and dry!

4. Banana as a Phone Stand

No phone stand? No problem! A perfectly ripe banana can serve as an emergency phone holder. You simply cut a small slit in the banana, adjust it until it stands up, and place your phone in the slice. Who knew fruit could be so multi-functional?
