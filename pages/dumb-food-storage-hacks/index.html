Dumb Food Storage Hacks

Introduction to Food Storage Hacks

Food storage is an essential part of maintaining a tidy kitchen and reducing waste. Here, we explore some pointless but funny food storage hacks that just might brighten your day. These hacks may not be the most efficient, but they sure are entertaining!

1. Using a Muffin Tin for Snacks

If you find yourself snacking too much from family-size bags of snacks, consider using a muffin tin! Simply fill each cup with a different snack like nuts, chocolate chips, or pretzels. Not only does it look fancy, but it also helps with portion control. How silly!

How to Set It Up

  • Pick a variety of your favorite snacks.
  • Use a clean muffin tin.
  • Fill each cup with a different treat.
  • Store in the fridge for a chilled snack option!

2. Using Binder Clips for Chip Bags

Who needs fancy chip clips when you can use binder clips? Simply fold your opened chip bag, and use a binder clip to secure it. It’s functional, and you can color-code your snacks by using different colored clips!

Steps to Clip It Right

  1. Open your bag of chips.
  2. Fold over the top to keep it fresh.
  3. Slide a binder clip around the fold.
  4. Enjoy your quirky food storage solution.

3. Ice Cube Tray for Herb Storage

Take your extra herbs, chop them up, and place them into an ice cube tray filled with water or olive oil. While this method is entertaining, the reality is making more of a mess than saving space. It's a good laugh, though!

How to Create Your Herb Cubes

  • Simply chop herbs into small bits.
  • Fill an ice cube tray with the chopped herbs mixed with oil or water.
  • Freeze it overnight and pop out whenever you need fresh herbs!

4. Use a Clothes Hanger for Nuts

Have a surplus of nuts? Grab a clothes hanger! Place zip-lock bags full of nuts over the hanger. This storage hack saves space and definitely provides a laugh! No need for a pantry shelf!

Setting It Up

  1. Ziploc your assorted nuts.
  2. Hang the zip-locked bags from the clothes hanger.
  3. Enjoy the convenience while chuckling at the oddity!

5. Using Empty Jars for Leftovers

Instead of throwing away your glass jars, use them to store leftovers! It’s a way of being planet-friendly and also will have you smiling every time you grab your leftover soup.

Jar Storage Tips

  • Clean out any empty glass jars.
  • Label each jar for easy reference.
  • Stack them in the fridge like a quirky mini pantry!
