5. Your Rights

As a user within the European Union, you have the following rights under GDPR:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of any data collected about you.

  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how your data is processed in certain situations.
  • Right to Object: Object to your data being processed.
  • Right to Data Portability: Request your data in a transferable format.

Since we do not collect personal data, these rights are generally not applicable. However, if you have concerns, feel free to contact us.
